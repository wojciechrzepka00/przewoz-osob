Safe Child Transport in Kraków and the Region — Car Seats and Regulations

Every year we drive thousands of children around Kraków and Małopolska — to school trips in Zakopane and the Pieniny, to the Wieliczka Salt Mine, to weddings in Podhale, and on transfers to Balice and Pyrzowice airports. Parents, teachers and event organisers who book a bus with us always ask the same question first: is the vehicle properly equipped for children, and who is responsible for the car seats? Here is what you should know before you book a minibus or coach in Kraków for a trip involving kids.

What Polish law says about child restraints

Under Polish traffic regulations, a child up to 150 cm in height must travel in an appropriate child restraint system matched to their weight and age — an infant carrier, a car seat, or a booster seat. This applies in passenger cars and in minibuses just as much as in a private car. The important nuance for organised transport concerns buses and coaches: children can be transported without dedicated child seats if the vehicle is designed and equipped for group passenger transport and seatbelts are available, which is the standard for our 8- and 20-seat buses and our coaches for up to around 50 passengers. In practice, this means a school trip from Kraków to Auschwitz-Birkenau or a group of wedding guests' children travelling from the Old Town to a venue near Wieliczka can be legally and safely transported in a seatbelt-equipped bus, supervised by teachers or parents.

For younger children, especially toddlers and infants, we recommend bringing your own certified car seat or booster whenever possible — parents know their child's seat best, and it travels with the family afterwards. If you need us to provide seats (for example for a wedding transfer where a family is arriving from out of town without their own equipment, or for a corporate event with an on-site nursery), let us know the number of children and their approximate age and weight at the time of booking. We will confirm availability and fit the seats securely before departure from your pick-up point in Kraków, Wieliczka, Skawina or wherever your journey begins.

How we keep young passengers safe on every Małopolska route

Booking a bus for children in Kraków is not only about the seat itself — it is about the whole journey. Our drivers are experienced with school groups and know the specifics of local routes: the mountain roads to Zakopane and the Tatras, the narrower lanes around Ojców National Park, and the busy city traffic near Rynek Główny and Kazimierz during peak tourist season.
