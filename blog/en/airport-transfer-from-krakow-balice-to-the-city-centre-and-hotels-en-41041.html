Airport Transfer from Kraków-Balice to the City Centre and Hotels

16.11.2026

Landing at John Paul II International Airport Kraków-Balice is usually just the beginning of the journey – whether you're heading to a hotel near the Main Square, a wedding venue in the Kraków countryside, or straight to a corporate meeting in the city. As a local transport company based in Kraków, we know this airport and the routes leading from it better than most, and we offer private transfers that take the stress out of arrival or departure, any time of day or night.

Kraków-Balice is located roughly 11-15 km from the city centre, depending on your exact destination, and under normal traffic conditions a transfer takes between 20 and 35 minutes. However, anyone who has driven along ulica Balicka or through the Bronowice junction during rush hour knows that travel times can stretch considerably, especially on weekday mornings and during events in the city. Our drivers monitor traffic on routes such as the Trasa Balicka, the Kraków ring road (Trasa Zwierzyniecka), and connections via Bronowice or Mydlniki, choosing the fastest option in real time rather than following a fixed route blindly.

Why book a private transfer instead of a taxi or shuttle

A private transfer from Legendary Kraków means the car or bus is waiting for your specific flight, not a scheduled departure time. We track incoming flights, so if your plane is delayed due to weather or air traffic, your driver adjusts automatically – no extra charges, no panic at the arrivals hall. This is particularly valuable for families with children, business travellers arriving late in the evening, or groups returning from a conference who simply want to get to their hotel without negotiating with taxi drivers at the terminal.

For larger groups – wedding guests arriving for a ceremony in Kraków or nearby in Małopolska, companies bringing employees in for a conference, or school groups returning from a trip – a single minibus or coach is far more practical and economical than several taxis. Our fleet includes air-conditioned 8-seat and 20-seat buses as well as coaches for up to around 50 passengers, so whether you're transferring two people or an entire wedding party, we match the vehicle to the group size.

Typical destinations and routes from Balice

Most of our airport transfers go to hotels in the Old Town, Kazimierz, Podgórze, and the area around Stary Kleparz and Nowa Huta, but we regularly drive guests further afield – to wedding venues near Niepołomice, Wieliczka, or in the Kraków-Częstochowa Upland, to conference centres such as ICE Kraków or venues near Czyżyny, and to business parks on the outskirts of the city. We also handle transfers in the opposite direction, from Kraków-Balice to Katowice-Pyrzowice airport, which is a common need for travellers connecting between the two airports for business trips or when flight schedules don't align.

Booking is straightforward: tell us your flight number, pickup or drop-off address, and the number of passengers with luggage, and we'll confirm the vehicle and driver. Our drivers wait at the arrivals area with a name sign, help with luggage, and know the layout of Balice well enough to find you quickly even during busy arrival periods when the terminal is crowded.

If you're organising transport for a wedding party arriving by plane, a company bringing staff in for an event, or simply need a reliable, pre-booked ride to your hotel after a long flight, a private transfer with a local Kraków-based driver removes the guesswork. We know which exits to use, which hotels have tricky parking or drop-off zones in the Old Town's restricted traffic area, and how to plan timing around Kraków's variable road conditions – all things a one
